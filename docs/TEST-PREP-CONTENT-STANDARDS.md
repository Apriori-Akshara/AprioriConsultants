# Apriori Test-Prep — Content Quality Benchmarks

**Created:** September 11, 2026  
**Status:** Benchmark document; governed by `docs/TEST-PREP-CONTENT-QUALITY-CONTROL.md`

This document defines the external benchmark hierarchy for Apriori SAT, GRE and GMAT content. It does **not** replace the master content-quality control document.

## 1. Benchmark hierarchy

### SAT — active priority

**Presentation / assessment experience:** College Board / Bluebook  
**Reading & Writing quality benchmark:** OnePrep primary; Princeton Review secondary  
**Math quality benchmark:** Kaplan primary; Barron's secondary

College Board establishes the structural and presentation reference. OnePrep is the principal benchmark for R&W breadth, skill-level drilling, question-bank depth, explanations and learning quality. Princeton Review is a secondary benchmark for realistic practice and strategy. Kaplan and Barron's are the principal benchmarks for Math rigor, variety, worked solutions, diagrams, data displays and problem construction.

### GRE — future planning only

**Official presentation:** ETS / POWERPREP  
**Content and instruction:** Magoosh

No GRE implementation is authorized by this benchmark document.

### GMAT — future planning only

**Official presentation:** GMAT Official materials/tests  
**Verbal:** Manhattan Prep  
**Quant:** GMAT Club plus validated official material

No GMAT implementation is authorized by this benchmark document.

## 2. Originality rule

External resources are benchmarks only.

Apriori must not copy:

- questions;
- passages;
- answer choices;
- explanations;
- diagrams;
- lessons;
- screenshots;
- proprietary visual assets;
- branding;
- proprietary scoring algorithms.

The objective is equivalent or superior educational quality through independently authored content.

## 3. SAT content target

Apriori SAT should combine:

- College Board-level structural fidelity;
- OnePrep-level R&W depth and taxonomy;
- Princeton Review-level instructional realism;
- Kaplan/Barron's-level Math rigor and variety;
- original Apriori pedagogy;
- visual learning;
- reliable explanations;
- adaptive-test realism;
- strong review and analytics readiness.

The SAT content program now includes a PSAT/SAT mock area with:

- 10 PSAT/NMSQT-aligned mocks;
- 10 SAT mocks — Series A;
- 10 SAT mocks — Series B.

Total initial full-length mock inventory: **30**.

## 4. Exercise and drill standard

Foundation:

- 5 sets;
- 10 questions per set;
- 50 questions per exercise/drill;
- major difficulty jump after every set.

Advanced:

- 10 sets;
- 20 questions per set;
- 200 questions per exercise/drill;
- major difficulty jump after every set.

Timed activities use product/section benchmark time-per-question to compute a hard timeout. Non-timed activities never time out but always record elapsed time.

These rules are governed by:

`docs/TEST-PREP-CONTENT-QUALITY-CONTROL.md`

## 5. SAT lessons standard

SAT lessons must be premium instructional content rather than passive text pages.

Target sequence:

**concept → visual explanation → worked example → strategy → guided practice → independent practice → error diagnosis → transfer**

Use graphics, interactive visuals, annotated equations, geometry diagrams, data-display walkthroughs, passage annotation and purposeful animation wherever these improve learning.

## 6. SAT calculator standard

The PSAT/SAT Math experience must provide an integrated calculator throughout Math, with question metadata indicating intended calculator use. Current College Board practice/testing supports embedded Desmos scientific and graphing options and prohibits CAS functionality.
